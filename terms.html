The app

Kept is a personal expense tracker. It is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, and it is not financial, tax, investment or legal advice. It does not move money and does not connect to your bank. Budgets, totals, reminders and alerts are for your information only. Kept is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.

Automatic logging depends on Apple’s Shortcuts app and on payment notifications from Wallet. Those depend on your device, iOS version and bank, so Kept cannot promise that every payment will be logged. Reminders and alerts are local notifications, and iOS may delay or block them (for example under Focus or if you turn notifications off). Receipt scanning reads text automatically and can make mistakes. Please check your records against your bank statements.
