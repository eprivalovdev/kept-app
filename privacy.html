Short version: the developer of Kept collects no data about you. Kept has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it also goes to your own private iCloud.

What Kept stores, and where

  • On your device. Your expenses and income (amount, currency, date, category, merchant, note and the card name shown in the Wallet notification), categories, budgets, recurring payments, people and splits, and settings are stored on your iPhone. Kept shares this data with its own widgets and its Apple Watch app on your devices only: through an App Group on the iPhone, and through Apple’s watch connection to your watch.
  • Receipt photos. If you scan a receipt, Kept keeps the photo and the item lines it read in its own storage on your iPhone, next to the expense. You can remove a receipt from the expense, and it is deleted with the expense. Receipt photos are not part of iCloud sync.
  • Optional iCloud sync (Kept Pro). If you turn it on, Kept stores your expenses, categories, budgets, recurring payments, people and splits in your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit. Only you can access it, and the developer cannot see it. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to that storage. Turn it off in Kept’s Settings.
  • Automatic logging. If you set it up, the Shortcuts app passes the text of a Wallet payment notification (card name, merchant, amount and date) to Kept, on your device. Kept does not read your card numbers and does not connect to your bank. Kept keeps a short log of its last 20 automation runs (time, route and result, not the payment details) so you can check that it works.
  • Receipt scanning. The camera and photo picker are used only when you scan a receipt. The text is read on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework. On iPhone models that support Apple Intelligence, Kept can also use Apple’s on-device language model to tidy the result. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any other server by Kept.
  • Splitting with friends. Kept stores the names you enter or pick for people and groups, what each person owes and what they paid back. If you pick someone from your contacts, you use the system contact picker and Kept keeps only the name and an identifier for that contact. Kept never sends anything to those people. “Remind” writes a message and hands it to the iOS share sheet, and you choose whether and where to send it.
  • Notifications. The notifications Kept shows (payment categories, reminders, budget alerts) are created locally on your iPhone. Kept uses no push service of its own. If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple’s own push service tells your devices that synced data changed.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account.
  • Face ID. If you turn on the lock, the check is done by iOS. Kept never receives your biometric data.
  • Feedback. If you choose Contact support in Kept, Kept opens an email to the developer in the Mail app. It contains the topics and note you typed and the app version, iOS version, language and region. It does not contain your amounts, merchants or categories. Nothing is sent until you send the email.

Network use

Kept itself does not call any server run by the developer or by a third party. Data leaves your iPhone only through Apple’s services: iCloud (if you turn sync on), the App Store (purchases and Restore Purchases) and the iOS share sheet and Mail when you use them. Links in the app open this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What the developer does not do

  • Does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • Does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not run a backend that stores your information.

Export and deletion

You can export your expenses as CSV (Kept Pro) and import a CSV (free). You can delete a transaction, budget, recurring payment, split or receipt inside Kept. Deleting the app removes the local data from that iPhone. Data in your private iCloud can be removed from Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Kept is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer does not run analytics here. GitHub may process standard server logs under its own privacy statement.
