Automatic logging

Set it up once.
Then just pay.

A one-time setup in the Shortcuts app takes a few minutes. After that, your Apple Pay payments reach Kept with their amount, merchant and card, and a notification asks for the category. One tap files it, right from the Lock Screen.

  1. iOS 27

    When Wallet notifies you

    The shortcut runs when Wallet sends its payment notification. Kept comes with the shortcut, ready to add.

  2. iOS 26

    When you tap to pay

    A Wallet automation runs as you pay with your card. The setup guide in Kept walks you through it, and its Test it button logs a sample payment, so you can check the setup without paying.

  3. Always

    When one slips by

    Some banks send no Wallet notification, and a shortcut can miss a payment now and then. Payments you have not filed yet wait in Needs a category. If the shortcut misses one, adding it by hand takes a few seconds.

Kept never sees your card number and never connects to your bank. It gets only what Shortcuts passes on from the payment: the amount, the merchant, the card name and the date.

Private by design

No account.
No bank login.
No ads.

Your data stays on your iPhone. There are no servers run by the developer, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

  • Optional iCloud sync (Kept Pro) uses your own private iCloud.
  • Double-tap the total to hide amounts when someone is looking.
  • This website, too: no cookies, no analytics, nothing loaded from elsewhere.
