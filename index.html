Spend, kept.

Pay with your iPhone, tap a category, and the expense is in your log. No account, no bank login, no ads.

Download on the App Store

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Watch app included.

How it works

You add one shortcut to Kept, once. After that, each card payment you make with your iPhone is logged by Kept, which asks you for a category. It relies on the Shortcuts app and on Wallet payment notifications, so a few banks and a few payments may not log on their own. You can always add an expense by hand.

What you get

  • A log of your spending by day, with search, filters and Insights.
  • Budgets, overall and by category, with what is left and a daily allowance.
  • Reminders before a recurring charge, when a budget runs low, and when automatic logging goes quiet. You choose which to keep, and Kept sends at most one scheduled reminder a day.
  • Recurring bills and subscriptions.
  • Receipt scanning with your camera, read on your iPhone.
  • Split a payment with friends and track who owes you.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and an Apple Watch app for quick adding.
  • CSV import and export, and a Face ID lock.

Kept is free to use with limits. Kept Pro adds more features and higher limits, with a monthly, yearly (7-day free trial) or one-time lifetime plan. See the Terms for prices.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server run by the developer. Optional iCloud sync (Kept Pro) uses your own private iCloud. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Read the Privacy Policy.
